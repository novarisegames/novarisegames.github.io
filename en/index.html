How it plays

Your realm, day by day

Move your heroes across the map, seize mines, cast spells and complete missions. Every day counts: when you end it, your rivals make their move too.

  • 🗺️
    Explore the map

    Push through the fog of war and discover castles, ruins, mines and creatures.

  • ⛏️
    Capture mines

    Every mine you hold gives you resources each day: gold, wood, ore and gems.

  • ⚔️
    Grow your heroes

    Attack, defense, mana, logistics and diplomacy. Every level makes them stronger.

  • ✨
    Cast spells

    Town Portal, Clairvoyance, Forced March… Magic can turn the tide of the game.

  • 🚩
    Missions and tutorial

    A guided tutorial and missions so you master your realm from day one.
