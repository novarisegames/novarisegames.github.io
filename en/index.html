    Help

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I try them before buying?

    Yes. Both games have a free demo in the browser: the first 5 days of Realms of Legend and 8 minutes of Tidebound, with no sign-up and nothing to download.

    Is Realms of Legend finished?

    Not yet. Realms of Legend is in active development and sold in early access: you can already play the 6-mission campaign, but we keep adding content, polishing the balance and fixing bugs. That's why it costs just $1.99.

    Where do I buy it and how do I get it?

    You buy it on our itch.io page. After paying, the game is added to your itch.io account (or you get a link by email if you buy without an account) and you can play or download it whenever you want.

    Which payment methods do you accept?

    Those offered by itch.io: credit or debit card and PayPal.

    Will I have to pay again for updates?

    No. It's a one-time payment: new versions we publish on itch.io will be available with your purchase.

    How do I hear about updates and new projects?

    Follow NovaRise Games on itch.io: you'll be notified whenever we publish a Realms of Legend update or a new game.
