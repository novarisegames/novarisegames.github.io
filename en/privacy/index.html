3. Providers involved

For the website to work, some data passes through these providers, each with its own privacy policy:

  • GitHub (GitHub Pages), which hosts the site. Like any server, it receives your IP address when serving pages. GitHub privacy statement.
  • GoatCounter, which counts visits without cookies and without storing IP addresses. GoatCounter privacy policy.
  • itch.io, which sells and delivers the games. itch.io privacy policy.
  • Google (Gmail), which handles the contact email.

Some of these providers are based in the United States or process data there. In those cases, transfers rely on the safeguards each provider offers under the GDPR. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else unless required by law.

4. Cookies

This website does not use cookies, first-party or third-party, and its typefaces are served from the site itself. The demos only save your progress in your browser's local storage, which is necessary for the function you request by playing.

5. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability at any time by writing to novarisegames26@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with the reply, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or with the supervisory authority of your country.

6. Children

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 14. If you are under 14 and need to write to us, ask a parent or guardian to contact us.

7. Changes to this policy

If the way we process data changes, we will update this page and its date.
