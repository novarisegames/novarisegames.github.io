Description

Seven realms fight for supremacy, from the Golden Oasis to the Crown of Ice, from the Blue Tower to the Burning Ash. In Realms of Legend you choose your realm, recruit its heroes and move your armies day by day across a map shrouded in fog of war: seize mines, cast spells, complete missions and conquer rival castles.

The game is in early access: it already includes a 6-mission campaign and keeps growing with every update.

Features

  • 7 realms, 14 heroes and 39 creatures
  • 7 maps to choose from
  • 6-mission campaign with a guided tutorial
  • Heroes with attack, defense, mana, logistics and diplomacy
  • Plays in the browser, no installation

Description

Survive on the open sea. In Tidebound your ship fires on its own: you just sail, collect the wood enemies drop and upgrade your vessel. Every level brings you closer to a new age, from a prehistoric log canoe to a hovering ship of the future, through storms, dark nights, treasure and monsters like the Kraken and the Leviathan.

Features

  • 7 ship ages: Prehistoric, Ancient, Medieval, Pirates, Industrial, Modern and Futuristic
  • Two seas: Tropical Sea and Northern Sea
  • Bosses: the Kraken and the Leviathan
  • Achievements and photo mode
  • Short sessions, great on mobile
